Mesa Zink for Habumi 0.3.0-rc.1
==============================

runtime/bin/opengl32.dll is the unmodified ARM64 Zink DLL from the
mmozeiko/build-mesa 26.2.4 release, not the Windows system OpenGL DLL.
Asset: mesa-zink-arm64-26.2.4.7z
Archive SHA256: aad83c138849348a6f70d8c50fd6a51b27af84ebbdf3fc93a204fde2c6c71b85
DLL SHA256: 36c6beacbd7059f088cb15fc05e460c61bf27a3981af59db1233a7e079ed8dea
Release and build scripts: https://github.com/mmozeiko/build-mesa/releases/tag/26.2.4
Mesa upstream: https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/mesa/mesa

The earlier Habumi D3D12 DLL used separate project patches against Mesa26.2.0.
Those patches are not the source of this Zink binary. The installed Qualcomm
driver is used at runtime and is not redistributed.
